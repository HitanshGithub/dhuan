# Dhuan (धुआं)

**Where is the stubble smoke going, and when does it reach you?**

Every October and November, farm fires in Punjab and Haryana send smoke across North India. Dhuan pulls
NASA's satellite fire detections and the hourly wind forecast every hour, simulates where each fire's smoke
travels over the next 48 hours, and tells you when it arrives at your home or school, when it peaks, how
heavy it is, and which districts it came from. Subscribe and you get a day's notice with an advisory in
Hindi, Punjabi or English.

Built solo during WeMakeDevs x AWS **Environmental Hacks** (8-11 Oct 2026), Air track. Work in progress.
